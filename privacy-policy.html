隐私政策

生效日期：2025年10月16日

欢迎使用 WordLearn（极光谷单词）！我们非常重视您的隐私保护。本隐私政策旨在帮助您了解我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。

重要提示： WordLearn 是一款基于 AI 图片识别的智能单词学习应用。我们仅在提供核心功能时收集必要的数据，并承诺不会向第三方出售或分享您的个人信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

📷相机和图片数据

  • 收集内容：您使用相机拍摄的图片
  • 使用目的：通过 AI 识别图片中的中英文单词，生成个性化学习内容
  • 存储方式：图片临时上传到服务器进行识别，处理完成后您可以选择删除
  • 权限说明：需要 android.permission.CAMERA 权限

💾本地存储数据

  • 收集内容：单词本、学习进度、关卡记录、用户偏好设置
  • 使用目的：保存您的学习数据，提供个性化学习体验
  • 存储方式：数据加密存储在您的设备本地
  • 权限说明：需要 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 和 WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 权限

🔊音频数据

  • 收集内容：单词发音音频
  • 使用目的：播放单词标准发音，辅助学习
  • 权限说明：需要 RECORD_AUDIO 和 MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS 权限

🌐网络数据

  • 收集内容：图片上传请求、AI 识别结果
  • 使用目的：与服务器通信，调用 AI 服务进行文字识别
  • 权限说明：需要 INTERNET 和 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE 权限

2. 我们如何使用您的信息

  • ✅ 学习关卡：生成多种类型的游戏化学习任务（单词配对、翻译练习等）
  • ✅ 进度跟踪：记录学习进度、准确率、经验值等数据
  • ✅ 功能优化：分析使用数据改进应用体验（匿名统计）

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2025

Welcome to WordLearn! We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

Important Notice: WordLearn is an AI-powered image recognition vocabulary learning application. We only collect necessary data to provide core functionality and promise never to sell or share your personal information with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

📷Camera and Photo Data

  • What We Collect: Photos you take with the camera
  • Purpose: Use AI to recognize Chinese and English words in images and generate personalized learning content
  • Storage: Photos are temporarily uploaded to servers for recognition and can be deleted after processing
  • Permissions: Requires android.permission.CAMERA permission

💾Local Storage Data

  • What We Collect: Vocabulary books, learning progress, level records, user preferences
  • Purpose: Save your learning data and provide personalized learning experience
  • Storage: Encrypted data stored locally on your device
  • Permissions: Requires READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

🔊Audio Data

  • What We Collect: Word pronunciation audio
  • Purpose: Play standard word pronunciations to assist learning
  • Permissions: Requires RECORD_AUDIO and MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

🌐Network Data

  • What We Collect: Image upload requests, AI recognition results
  • Purpose: Communicate with servers and invoke AI services for text recognition
  • Permissions: Requires INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

2. How We Use Your Information

  • ✅ AI Text Recognition: Use Doubao Vision API or Baidu OCR to recognize text in images
  • ✅ Generate Vocabulary: Automatically extract Chinese and English words to create personalized learning content
  • ✅ Learning Levels: Generate various types of gamified learning tasks (word matching, translation practice, etc.)
  • ✅ Progress Tracking: Record learning progress, accuracy, experience points, and other data
  • ✅ Feature Optimization: Analyze usage data to improve app experience (anonymous statistics)

3. Third-Party Services

To provide core functionality, we use the following third-party services:
